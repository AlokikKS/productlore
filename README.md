# ProductLore

### Not company history. Product thinking.

ProductLore is an interactive storytelling platform exploring how some of the world's greatest products came to exist.

Instead of simply reading company histories, users can:

- Explore cinematic product journeys
- Understand the problem behind the product
- See how the first MVP evolved
- Make historical product decisions
- Compare their decisions with what actually happened
- Capture their own product ideas in a personal Product Notebook

## Product Philosophy

ProductLore is built around a simple idea:

> Understanding why a product won is more useful than simply knowing that it won.

The goal is to help aspiring Product Managers, founders, designers, and builders develop product intuition through real product journeys.
